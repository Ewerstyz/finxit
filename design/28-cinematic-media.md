## Prompts finais

### finxi-neighborhood

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen opening photograph for FinxiIT company presentation, camera zooms inward on scroll. Primary request: cinematic editorial photograph of a real-looking Brazilian neighborhood commercial street at blue hour, human-scale independent shops, a modest ceramics and homeware shop with rich cobalt blue wooden door centrally framed as the destination for an inward camera move. Wide landscape 16:9. Street architecture everyday Brazil: low plaster storefronts, tactile painted walls, warm interior lamps, sidewalks, some foliage. View from across narrow street with foreground doorway framing softly at extreme edges, clear depth from street to open cobalt shop doorway. Store entrance and worktable visible near center-right, quiet photographic realism, warm amber light inside contrasted with indigo evening outside. A couple of naturally distant people, small neighborhood businesses. Leave the upper central sky/wall and upper left relatively uncluttered for large white webpage heading. Precise believable perspective, premium cinema production still, no futuristic neon, no skyscrapers, no wealth stereotype or poverty drama. No rendered letters, no logos, no fake signage, no watermark. The image represents entrepreneurial context, not an actual company headquarters.

### finxi-workspace

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic closer view for FinxiIT company website camera zoom story. Primary request: natural editorial close-up of a small Brazilian entrepreneur's everyday worktable inside a modest ceramics and homeware shop. Landscape 16:9. Warm worn wooden table, open modest notebook with indistinct handwritten lines, a few ordinary paper receipts with unreadable marks, a pen, ceramic objects at the edge, smartphone resting screen down with a dark cobalt blue case. Adult Black Brazilian woman's hand gently holding the pen over notebook, realistic anatomy, only hand/forearm visible not face. Late afternoon blue door light and amber desk light, beautifully tactile, subtle depth of field but notebook and phone both recognizable. Composition table objects primarily center/right with dark softly blurred shop background in upper left allowing white site text. Feels like calm practical thinking after a busy business day, authentic no luxury set. No visible app UI, no readable text, no bank cards, no money bundles, no logos, no watermark. High-end documentary/cinema still.
